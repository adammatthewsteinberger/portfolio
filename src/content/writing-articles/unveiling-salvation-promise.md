## Every religion says, "Do this, and you'll be saved."

Judaism: Keep the Law.

Islam: Follow the Five Pillars.

Buddhism: Achieve enlightenment.

Hinduism: Fulfill your karma.

**Christianity says the opposite:**

"You can't do enough. So Yeshua did it for you."

## Good Works Don't Save You

"For by grace you have been saved through faith—and this is not from yourselves, it is the gift of G-d—not by works, so that no one can boast." (Ephesians 2:8-9)

Salvation isn't a reward. It's a **gift.**

You don't earn gifts. You receive them.

## Isaiah Saw It

"All of us have become like one who is unclean, and all our righteous acts are like filthy rags." (Isaiah 64:6)

Even your best efforts fall short.

**That's why you need a Savior.**

Not a coach. Not a teacher. Not a role model.

Someone who can do for you what you can't do for yourself.

## Yeshua Did What You Couldn't

He lived the perfect life you couldn't live.

He died the death you deserved.

He rose from the grave to prove it worked.

**Now salvation is offered freely to anyone who receives it.**

## So Stop Trying to Earn It

You can't be good enough. Religious enough. Moral enough.

**You just have to trust Him.**

Receive the gift. Let Him save you.

That's it.
