## "If anyone is thirsty, let him come to Me and drink."

**John 7:37**

You've climbed the ladder. Checked the boxes. Tried the self-help books, the meditation apps, the therapy.

And still, something's missing.

**Why?**

Because you're drinking from broken cisterns. Sources that can't satisfy.

## Isaiah Saw This Coming

"Why spend money on what is not bread, and your labor on what does not satisfy?" (Isaiah 55:2)

The prophets knew. Human achievements, earthly pleasures, even religious performance: none of it fills the void.

**Only G-d can.**

And Yeshua claimed to be the source.

## "Living Water" Isn't a Metaphor

Yeshua promised that if you drink from Him, you'll **never thirst again.**

Not because life gets easier. But because your soul gets satisfied.

He offers something the world can't give: **peace that doesn't depend on circumstances.**

Purpose that doesn't fade. Hope that doesn't disappoint.

## The Invitation Is Still Open

You don't have to earn it. You don't have to clean yourself up first.

Yeshua said, "Let anyone who is thirsty come."

**Anyone.**

That includes you.

So why are you still drinking from dry wells?
