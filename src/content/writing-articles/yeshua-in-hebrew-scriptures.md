## Yeshua isn't just in the Gospels. He's in Genesis. Exodus. Psalms. Isaiah. Everywhere.

**The Passover Lamb** (Exodus 12): An unblemished lamb dies so the people can live. Sound familiar?

**The Suffering Servant** (Isaiah 53): Pierced for our transgressions. Crushed for our iniquities.

**The Son of David** (Psalm 2, 110): A king who rules forever.

**The Prophet Like Moses** (Deuteronomy 18): Someone G-d would raise up to speak His words.

**The Seed of Abraham** (Genesis 22): Through whom all nations would be blessed.

## It's Not Subtle

The Hebrew Bible doesn't hint at the Messiah.

It **screams** about Him.

Over 300 specific prophecies. Patterns. Types. Shadows.

All pointing to one person.

## And Yeshua Claimed It All

He said, "Moses wrote about Me." (John 5:46)

He said, "The Scriptures testify about Me." (John 5:39)

He didn't just show up and say, "Hey, I'm the Messiah."

He **fulfilled** the Scriptures. In detail. Over and over.

## Once You See It...

You can't unsee it.

The Passover lamb becomes more than a ritual.

Isaiah 53 becomes more than poetry.

The whole Hebrew Bible becomes a **roadmap to Yeshua.**

## So Read It Again

Not as disconnected stories.

But as one unified story, pointing to one Redeemer.

**Yeshua.**
