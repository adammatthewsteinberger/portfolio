Hello and welcome to **The Autistic Apologist**! I'm [Adam Matthew](https://www.instagram.com/realadammatthew), and I'm excited to share this space with you.

## About These Essays

This collection is a personal project where I explore the intersections of faith, neurodiversity, science, and technology. As both an autistic individual and a Christian apologist, I bring a unique perspective to discussions about theology, philosophy, and the world around us.

## What to Expect

You'll find essays covering a wide range of topics:

- **Autism and Neurodiversity**: Personal experiences and insights from an autistic perspective
- **Christian Apologetics**: Defending and explaining the Christian faith
- **Theology and Scripture**: Deep dives into biblical texts and theological concepts
- **Science and Technology**: Exploring physics, AI, software, and space exploration
- **Cultural Commentary**: Thoughts on American culture and society

## My Special Interests

One of the characteristics of autism is having intense special interests. Mine include:

- Mars and space exploration
- Physics and cosmology
- Artificial intelligence
- Software development
- Science fiction

You'll likely see these topics woven throughout my essays, as they inform how I view the world and approach bigger questions.

## Join the Journey

Whether you're here for the theology, the science, the autism advocacy, or just curious about how these topics intersect, I hope you'll find something valuable. Feel free to explore, read, and engage with the content.

Thank you for being here!
